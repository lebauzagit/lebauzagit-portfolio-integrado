About
I’m a full stack developer focused on backend engineering and software architecture. My frontend background helps me connect what users do in an interface with how the underlying APIs, data, and background jobs support it.

I work with TypeScript, Node.js, and NestJS on the backend, and with Vue, Nuxt, and Astro on the frontend. My work spans REST APIs, data persistence, and asynchronous processing with PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, and BullMQ.

For AIMove Hub, I collaborated with the frontend team on a transportation and logistics platform and contributed to its documentation for web and mobile users. For YLG Studio Nails, I built a bilingual Astro website that connects a service catalog and photo gallery with online booking.
