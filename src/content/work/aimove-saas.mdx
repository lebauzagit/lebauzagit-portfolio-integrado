## Frontend Collaboration on AIMove Hub

I collaborated with the frontend team on **AIMove Hub**, a SaaS platform for transportation and logistics. I contributed to interfaces for operations, maintenance, safety, and finance, including dashboards and reporting views used across the platform.

The project brought several teams and workflows into one application. My work centered on the frontend experience and the shared interface patterns that helped those modules feel like parts of the same product.
