## Collaboration on the AIMove Hub User Guide

I collaborated on the **AIMove Hub user guide**, a Nuxt documentation site for the transportation and logistics platform. I contributed to the structure of the site and to guides that explain how to navigate the applications and carry out common tasks.

The team organized the documentation by application and user role. Web users can find guidance for their module, while drivers and technicians have separate material for the workflows they use in the field.
