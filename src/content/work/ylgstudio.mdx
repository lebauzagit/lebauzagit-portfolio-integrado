## Building a bilingual booking website with Astro

I built the public-facing website for **YLG Studio Nails**, a manicure and pedicure studio in Brandon, Florida. A new client might arrive with a design in mind, a budget, or a free afternoon. I organized the site so they could start with photos, compare treatments by price and duration, then check availability in English or Spanish.

The development challenge was keeping an image-heavy site easy to browse and a bilingual catalog easy to maintain. With Astro, I could compose the pages from reusable components and use TypeScript for shared service data. That lets the studio feature a treatment on the homepage, link to its full listing, and describe it to search engines using the same underlying price.

## What the site delivers

A client can see examples of the studio’s work, find the right service, and reach the booking platform with enough information to choose an appointment. The studio can also publish articles and link readers back to the catalog or contact page. Those paths share the same navigation and visual language across both locales.
